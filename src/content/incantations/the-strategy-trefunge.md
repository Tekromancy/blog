The Strategy pattern allows a tekromancer to forge a Vector Matrix—a series of parallel execution corridors within the Three-Dimensional Topology Wards. 

Depending on the spectral context, the instruction pointer is guided into one of several isolated algorithms (`A`, `B`, etc.). Because these strategies inhabit the same dimensional bounds, they are completely interchangeable, providing immense tactical flexibility in the cyber-realm.
