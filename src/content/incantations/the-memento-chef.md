# The Memento

In the darkest kitchens of the cyberpunk underworld, the **Memento** is not merely coded; it is *cooked*. This profane recipe binds the principles of the Behavioral pattern into a physical manifestation of culinary dread. 

## Arcane Implementation

Experimentation in the dark arts often leads to explosive failure. The Memento pattern allows the chef to save the state of a fragile abomination before attempting a risky maneuver. The state is extracted and preserved in formaldehyde (the Memento object). The Caretaker holds the jar safely, unaware of its horrifying contents, until the Originator requests to revert to its former, un-ruined self.

- **Fragile Memory**: The state to be saved.
- **Preserved Brain**: The Memento object.
- **Jar of Formaldehyde**: The Caretaker holding the Memento.

*Consume at your own risk.*
