# The Composite Pattern

In polite necromancy, `Composite` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `Composite` pattern ensures your skeletal amalgamations can be treated uniformly, whether single bone or entire swarm, with the utmost courtesy.
