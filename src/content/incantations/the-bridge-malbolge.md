# The Bridge

The Bridge pattern tears the mind from the body, allowing the abstraction of terror to vary independently from its physical manifestation. As the eldritch entity grows in complexity, the bridge keeps the dimensional axes orthogonal, maintaining a terrifyingly elegant architecture of despair.
