# The Composite

The many are one, and the one is suffering. The Composite pattern structures horrors into tree-like hierarchies, allowing clients to interact with individual anomalies or a collective swarm of doom with uniform apathy. A singular command reverberates down the grotesque branches until everything screams in unison.
