# The Abstract Factory Pattern

In polite necromancy, `Abstract Factory` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `Abstract Factory` pattern ensures your undead entities are grouped and constructed with the utmost courtesy.
