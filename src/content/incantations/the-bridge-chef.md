# The Bridge

In the darkest kitchens of the cyberpunk underworld, the **Bridge** is not merely coded; it is *cooked*. This profane recipe binds the principles of the Structural pattern into a physical manifestation of culinary dread. 

## Arcane Implementation

The Bridge pattern severs the physical from the ethereal. Instead of a single monolithic monstrosity that is hard to alter, the Bridge creates a rift: on one side lies the high-level control logic (the abstraction), and on the other lies the writhing, low-level platform code (the implementation). They communicate across the void, allowing the master chef to swap out the underlying meat without changing the presentation.

- **Pure Abstraction**: The client-facing interface.
- **Dripping Implementation**: The concrete operations.
- **Spatial Rift**: The composition link between them.

*Consume at your own risk.*
