The Template Method acts as the Arcane Skeleton for complex rituals. The invariant steps of the Three-Dimensional Topology Ward are hardcoded into the base grid layer. 

When a specialized or subclass operation is required, the pointer is shifted into a Z-axis hook (`h`). This allows subclasses to define custom behaviors without compromising the overarching topological structure of the spell.
