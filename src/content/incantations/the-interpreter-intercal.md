# The Interpreter Pattern

In polite necromancy, `Interpreter` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `Interpreter` pattern ensures your eldritch whispers are systematically deciphered into executable curses with the utmost courtesy.
