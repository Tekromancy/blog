# The Chain of Responsibility

A request for mercy is submitted to the void, but no single entity handles it. The Chain of Responsibility pattern strings together multiple grotesque handlers. Each handler examines the plea, exacts its toll, and carelessly forwards it to the next until the request is entirely consumed or discarded.
