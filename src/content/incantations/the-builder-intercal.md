# The Builder Pattern

In polite necromancy, `Builder` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `Builder` pattern ensures your skeletal constructs are assembled step-by-step with the utmost courtesy.
