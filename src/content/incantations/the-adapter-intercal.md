# The Adapter Pattern

In polite necromancy, `Adapter` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `Adapter` pattern ensures mismatched flesh is properly grafted together with the utmost courtesy.
