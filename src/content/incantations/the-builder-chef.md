# The Builder

In the darkest kitchens of the cyberpunk underworld, the **Builder** is not merely coded; it is *cooked*. This profane recipe binds the principles of the Creational pattern into a physical manifestation of culinary dread. 

## Arcane Implementation

The Builder pattern separates the construction of a complex culinary horror from its representation. You do not simply mix everything at once; you methodically build the abomination layer by layer. The Director (the Master Chef) dictates the order of the steps, while the Builder (the sous-chef automaton) executes the bone-shaping algorithms.

- **Ground Marrow**: The structural framework of the dish.
- **Blood of the Innocent**: The binding agent that holds the complex object together.
- **Powdered Bone**: The finishing garnish that defines the final shape.
