# The Flyweight

In the darkest kitchens of the cyberpunk underworld, the **Flyweight** is not merely coded; it is *cooked*. This profane recipe binds the principles of the Structural pattern into a physical manifestation of culinary dread. 

## Arcane Implementation

Summoning a million distinct demonic locusts would drain the kitchen's memory reserves instantly. The Flyweight pattern solves this by extracting the intrinsic, immutable essence (the soul fragment) and sharing it across all instances. The locusts themselves are mere hollow shells that rely on extrinsic data (their specific coordinates in the swarm) passed in at runtime, allowing the endless swarm to blot out the sun without triggering an OutOfMemory spell failure.

- **Shared Soul Fragment**: The intrinsic state (Flyweight object).
- **Hollow Locust Shells**: The sheer volume of instances.
- **Extrinsic State**: Coordinates and context passed by the client.
