# The Factory Method

In the darkest kitchens of the cyberpunk underworld, the **Factory Method** is not merely coded; it is *cooked*. This profane recipe binds the principles of the Creational pattern into a physical manifestation of culinary dread. 

## Arcane Implementation

The Factory Method lets a class defer instantiation to subclasses. In the culinary underworld, the Base Recipe calls for a generic "abomination meat", but the specialized dark kitchens (subclasses) decide whether to provide coagulated vitae, ground marrow, or spectral ectoplasm. The main method remains pure, oblivious to the horrific specifics of the ingredients injected into its flow.

- **Coagulated Vitae**: The raw material instantiated by the subclass.
- **Shattered Dreams**: The logic modifier.
- **Salt of the Earth**: The grounding element to keep the magic stable.
