# The Prototype

In the darkest kitchens of the cyberpunk underworld, the **Prototype** is not merely coded; it is *cooked*. This profane recipe binds the principles of the Creational pattern into a physical manifestation of culinary dread. 

## Arcane Implementation

Why build a culinary abomination from scratch when you can simply clone an existing, perfectly horrifying dish? The Prototype pattern in Chef is represented by the dark art of mimicry. A master dish is placed in the center of the arcane circle, and through duplicating powders and unformed slime, exact replicas are spawned, preserving the internal state and torment of the original without the overhead of re-cooking.

- **Pure Mimicry**: The core interface for cloning.
- **Unformed Slime**: The uninitialized memory space.
- **Duplicating Powder**: The deep-copy mechanism.
