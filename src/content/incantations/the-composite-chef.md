# The Composite

In the darkest kitchens of the cyberpunk underworld, the **Composite** is not merely coded; it is *cooked*. This profane recipe binds the principles of the Structural pattern into a physical manifestation of culinary dread. 

## Arcane Implementation

The Composite pattern allows you to treat a single abomination and a writhing horde of abominations uniformly. By linking individual flesh-nodes into a recursive tree structure via psychic webbing, the chef can issue a single command ("Scream") to the root node, and the entire hive will cascade the method call down to the smallest ganglionic cluster.

- **Master Brain**: The root composite component.
- **Drone Ganglia**: The leaf nodes.
- **Psychic Webbing**: The list of child components.

*Consume at your own risk.*
