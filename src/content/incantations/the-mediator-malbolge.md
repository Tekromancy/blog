# The Mediator

When the myriad forces of the underworld clash, chaos reigns. The Mediator pattern imposes a terrifying order, serving as the central hub of communication. Objects are forbidden from interacting directly; all malice must pass through the Mediator, which dictates the complex choreography of doom.
