The Composite pattern allows tekromancers to treat individual nodes and entire branches of Three-Dimensional Topology Wards uniformly. By weaving fractal geometries through recursive Z-axis shifting (`h` and `l`), we create volumetric trees.

An instruction pointer can seamlessly traverse a single leaf ward or an entire sub-matrix without altering its dimensional trajectory algorithm.
