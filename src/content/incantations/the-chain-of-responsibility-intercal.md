# The Chain of Responsibility Pattern

In polite necromancy, `Chain of Responsibility` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `Chain of Responsibility` pattern ensures your haunting requests pass seamlessly from one specter to the next with the utmost courtesy.
