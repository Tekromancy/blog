When deep within the grid, recreating a complex Three-Dimensional Topology Ward from scratch costs precious cycles. The Prototype pattern enables Volumetric Cloning by duplicating the instruction stack (`:`) and shifting it across adjacent Z-planes (`h`, `l`).

This allows the tekromancer to spawn exact topological replicas of a ward, ready to be slightly mutated for their specific cyber-defense needs.
