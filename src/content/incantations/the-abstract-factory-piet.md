In the realm of Chromatic Sorcery, the Abstract Factory manifests as a canvas of sprawling hues. It establishes a broad spectrum of creation where groups of related color-blocks are birthed without specifying their exact coordinates. Mastery of this pattern allows the Weaver of Pixels to summon vast interlocked systems of logic.
