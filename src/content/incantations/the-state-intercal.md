# The State Pattern

In polite necromancy, `State` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `State` pattern ensures a corpse alters its behavior internally as rigor mortis progresses, acting with the utmost courtesy.
