In the depths of the neon-soaked arcane matrix, the Abstract Factory manifests as a Three-Dimensional Topology Ward. By utilizing the `h` (high) and `l` (low) dimensional shifts in Trefunge, a tekromancer can weave families of interrelated constructs across the Z-axis without exposing the underlying geometry of their creation.

This ward ensures that our cyber-spirits and data-daemons remain decoupled from their spatial instantiation constraints.
