# The Factory Method Pattern

In polite necromancy, `Factory Method` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `Factory Method` pattern ensures your soul-bindings are delegated to the appropriate subclass with the utmost courtesy.
