In dense arcane matrices, the memory limits of the grid become a bottleneck. The Flyweight pattern applies Resonance Compression to Three-Dimensional Topology Wards.

Instead of duplicating the geometry of multiple identical wards, we store the intrinsic state in a central high-density planar cluster (`p`). Extrinsic data is fetched dynamically (`g`) as the pointer passes through, maintaining massive wards with a minimal spatial footprint.
