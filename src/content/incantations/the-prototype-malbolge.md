# The Prototype

There is a unique terror in repetition. The Prototype pattern allows an eldritch entity to clone itself, bypassing the prohibitive cost of initial summoning. The exact memory states are copied, creating a fractal expansion of doom that mirrors its progenitor down to the darkest atom.
