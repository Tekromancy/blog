In the shifting topology of the arcane matrix, ancient 2D wards often fail to interface with modern Three-Dimensional Topology Wards. The Adapter acts as a Dimensional Translator.

By enveloping the legacy construct within an adapter shell, we shift the instruction pointer up (`h`) to translate the data into volumetric coordinates before shifting back down (`l`) to interface smoothly.
