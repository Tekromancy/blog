# The Decorator

In the darkest kitchens of the cyberpunk underworld, the **Decorator** is not merely coded; it is *cooked*. This profane recipe binds the principles of the Structural pattern into a physical manifestation of culinary dread. 

## Arcane Implementation

Subclassing is a rigid, permanent mutation. The Decorator pattern offers a more flexible approach: parasitism. By wrapping a base culinary entity within a symbiotic, chitinous shell, you grant it new properties—such as increased defense or toxic flavors—without altering its core structure. Multiple layers of parasites can be stacked, each adding its own dark behavior to the invocation chain.

- **Bare Host Flesh**: The concrete component.
- **Chitinous Parasites**: The decorator classes.
- **Acidic Glue**: The constructor injection binding them.

*Consume at your own risk.*
