# The Template Method Pattern

In polite necromancy, `Template Method` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `Template Method` pattern ensures your overarching reanimation algorithms remain intact while allowing ghoulish variations in the details, applied with the utmost courtesy.
