# The Abstract Factory

In the darkest kitchens of the cyberpunk underworld, the **Abstract Factory** is not merely coded; it is *cooked*. This profane recipe binds the principles of the Creational pattern into a physical manifestation of culinary dread. By consuming the resulting dish, the architecture of the pattern is inscribed directly into the neural pathways of the imbiber, overwriting their understanding of system design with eldritch geometries.

## Arcane Implementation

The Chef language interprets the physical act of cooking as computational execution. The ingredients represent our variables and state, while the method describes the transformation of these dark elements. 
The Abstract Factory acts as the master chef's grimoire, providing an interface to birth families of related horrors (e.g., tentacle-based appetizers vs. flesh-based entrees) without specifying the concrete abominations until mealtime.

- **Cursed Souls**: The fundamental data structures, screaming in the void.
- **Liquid Shadows**: The fluid control flow, seeping through the cracks of logic.
- **Despair**: The error-handling mechanism. When all else fails, despair is folded in.

*Consume at your own risk.*
