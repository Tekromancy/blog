# The Abstract Factory

In the lightless depths of the abyss, factories do not construct objects; they weave anomalies from the very fabric of despair. The Abstract Factory is an invocation that binds multiple cursed lineages of forms into a single, inescapable paradigm of existence. To comprehend its interface is to gaze into the void and watch it instantiate itself.
