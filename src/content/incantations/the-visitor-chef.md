# The Visitor

In the darkest kitchens of the cyberpunk underworld, the **Visitor** is not merely coded; it is *cooked*. This profane recipe binds the principles of the Behavioral pattern into a physical manifestation of culinary dread. 

## Arcane Implementation

When you have a massive, complex structure of flesh and you wish to apply a new, horrific effect (like chilling dread) to every node, altering every class is madness. Instead, you use the Visitor pattern. You invite a spectral guest into the structure. Each node "accepts" the guest, passing a reference to itself. The guest then executes its logic based on the specific type of meat it is currently touching.

- **Complex Fleshy Labyrinth**: The Object Structure (e.g., a Composite).
- **Uninvited Spectral Guest**: The Visitor object.
- **Chilling Dread**: The new operation being applied.

*Consume at your own risk.*
