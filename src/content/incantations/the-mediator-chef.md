# The Mediator

In the darkest kitchens of the cyberpunk underworld, the **Mediator** is not merely coded; it is *cooked*. This profane recipe binds the principles of the Behavioral pattern into a physical manifestation of culinary dread. 

## Arcane Implementation

When rival demons in the kitchen attempt to communicate directly, chaotic coupling and explosions of gore follow. The Mediator pattern acts as the central altar of the blood pact. The demons (colleagues) speak only to the altar, which routes their dark energies and coordinates the complex interactions between them. This maintains loose coupling in an environment prone to violent dismemberment.

- **Central Altar**: The Mediator object.
- **Rival Demons**: The Colleague objects.
- **Sacrificial Blood**: The events passed between them.

*Consume at your own risk.*
