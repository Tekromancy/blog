# The Observer

In the darkest kitchens of the cyberpunk underworld, the **Observer** is not merely coded; it is *cooked*. This profane recipe binds the principles of the Behavioral pattern into a physical manifestation of culinary dread. 

## Arcane Implementation

A central component (the subject) mutates, and its dark masters must know instantly. The Observer pattern embeds a network of floating, bloodshot eyeballs throughout the kitchen, all psychically linked to the beating heart of the subject. When the subject's state changes, a wave of psychic resonance is emitted, forcing all attached eyeballs to twitch in unison, executing their update logic.

- **Beating Heart of the Subject**: The Observable.
- **Floating Eyeballs**: The Observers.
- **Psychic Resonance**: The `notify()` mechanism.
