# The Decorator Pattern

In polite necromancy, `Decorator` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `Decorator` pattern ensures your flesh enhancements can be wrapped around base corpses dynamically with the utmost courtesy.
