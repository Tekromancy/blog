# The Interpreter

In the darkest kitchens of the cyberpunk underworld, the **Interpreter** is not merely coded; it is *cooked*. This profane recipe binds the principles of the Behavioral pattern into a physical manifestation of culinary dread. 

## Arcane Implementation

When the ingredients come labeled in the languages of the Old Ones, a standard compiler will fail. The Interpreter pattern constructs an Abstract Syntax Tree of madness. By combining syntax dust and a dictionary of horrors, the recipe iterates over the forbidden runes, mapping linguistic constructs to culinary actions, executing commands directly from the text of the damned.

- **String of Ancient Text**: The abstract expression.
- **Dictionary of Madness**: The context/terminal expressions.
- **Syntax Dust**: The non-terminal rule combinations.
