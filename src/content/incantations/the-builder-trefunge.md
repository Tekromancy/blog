The Builder pattern in Trefunge operates as a literal Z-Axis Scaffold. Complex Three-Dimensional Topology Wards require precise layer-by-layer construction to ensure that magical resonance doesn't collapse the instruction pointer's path. 

By defining distinct topological subroutines and traversing upwards (`h`), we step through the ritual until the final volumetric spell is complete, then traverse back down (`l`) to return the constructed entity.
