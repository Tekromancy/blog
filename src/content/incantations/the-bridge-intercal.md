# The Bridge Pattern

In polite necromancy, `Bridge` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `Bridge` pattern ensures the separation of a soul from its phylactery implementation with the utmost courtesy.
