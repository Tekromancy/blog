# The Proxy Pattern

In polite necromancy, `Proxy` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `Proxy` pattern ensures your thralls stand in for their master until access is strictly required, operating with the utmost courtesy.
