The Singleton is forged as a Monolith Node—a fixed anchor in the Three-Dimensional Topology Wards. Using Trefunge's spatial `g` (get) and `p` (put) commands, we check the multi-dimensional grid to see if the anchor is already set.

If it exists, the spell pointer bounces back, ensuring the fragile planar resonance is never disrupted by twin singularities.
