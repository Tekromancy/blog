# The Facade

In the darkest kitchens of the cyberpunk underworld, the **Facade** is not merely coded; it is *cooked*. This profane recipe binds the principles of the Structural pattern into a physical manifestation of culinary dread. 

## Arcane Implementation

Behind the counter of the dark kitchen lies a chaotic, writhing mass of interconnected subsystems: meat grinders, soul extractors, and blood centrifuges. The Facade pattern slaps a pleasant, smiling mask over this nightmare. It offers a simple, unified interface to the diner, shielding them from the grotesque complexity required to prepare their meal. They call `orderFood()`, blissfully unaware of the horrors that method triggers.

- **Pleasant Visage**: The simplified interface class.
- **Underlying Chaos**: The complex subsystems.
- **Sweet Perfume**: Syntactic sugar.

*Consume at your own risk.*
