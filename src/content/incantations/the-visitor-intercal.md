# The Visitor Pattern

In polite necromancy, `Visitor` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `Visitor` pattern ensures demonic entities can possess different flesh constructs without altering their internal structures, executed with the utmost courtesy.
