# The Command Pattern

In polite necromancy, `Command` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `Command` pattern ensures your dark incantations are encapsulated as objects for later execution with the utmost courtesy.
