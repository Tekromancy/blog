# The Strategy

In the darkest kitchens of the cyberpunk underworld, the **Strategy** is not merely coded; it is *cooked*. This profane recipe binds the principles of the Behavioral pattern into a physical manifestation of culinary dread. 

## Arcane Implementation

To butcher a resilient piece of meat, one cannot rely on a single approach. The Strategy pattern defines a family of butchering algorithms, each encapsulated within a specific cursed weapon. The master chef dynamically selects the tool (the Strategy) at runtime based on the meat's armor class, allowing the slicing algorithm to vary independently from the hand that wields it.

- **Hunk of Resistant Meat**: The Context.
- **Serrated Blade / Jagged Saw / Rusty Cleaver**: The concrete Strategies.
- **Chosen Tool**: The strategy interface injection.

*Consume at your own risk.*
