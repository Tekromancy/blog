The Bridge pattern functions as a Planar Decoupler within the Three-Dimensional Topology Wards. By placing the abstraction on a high plane (`h`) and the implementation on a low plane (`l`), the two geometries can evolve independently.

A tekromancer can swap out the implementation layer without disturbing the highly sensitive abstraction matrix above it.
