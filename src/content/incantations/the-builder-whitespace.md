# The Builder of the Null Architecture

When constructing complex artifacts of sheer nothingness, the Builder pattern guides the shaping of the void step by step. Each Space and Tab acts as a chisel against the backdrop of memory, slowly carving out the structure in the ethereal plane.

The Unseen Sigils provide no visual feedback; you must hold the entire structure in your mind's eye. A single misplaced Tab will shatter the silent construct. This is the ultimate test of a Transmuter's discipline.

We use the heap to store partial constructions, pushing and popping pointers with absolute silence until the final form is summoned into existence.
