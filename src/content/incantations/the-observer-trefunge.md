The Observer pattern creates a Resonance Web within the grid. Instead of a single point-to-point connection, an event at the nexus triggers a downward cascade through the Three-Dimensional Topology Wards.

As the instruction pointer drops (`l`), it spawns threads (`:`) or passes signals to subscribed wards that lie on different Z-planes (`h`). This ensures that an entire network of cyber-defenses reacts instantly to a singular breach.
