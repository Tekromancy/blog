# The Observer Pattern

In polite necromancy, `Observer` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `Observer` pattern ensures all bound spirits are notified whenever the scrying target changes state, accomplished with the utmost courtesy.
