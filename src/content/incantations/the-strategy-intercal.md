# The Strategy Pattern

In polite necromancy, `Strategy` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `Strategy` pattern ensures you can dynamically swap rituals during an active incantation with the utmost courtesy.
