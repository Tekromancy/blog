# The Mediator Pattern

In polite necromancy, `Mediator` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `Mediator` pattern ensures your seances channel all ethereal chatter through a single medium with the utmost courtesy.
