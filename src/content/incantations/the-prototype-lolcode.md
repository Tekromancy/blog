  PRIME_CAT HAS A HASH ITZ "0xCA7"

  I HAS A SHADOW_CAT ITZ I IZ CLONE_CAT YR PRIME_CAT MKAY
  VISIBLE "SHADOW DATA: " SHADOW_CAT'Z DATA

The Prototype spell allows a cyber-mage to duplicate an existing feline entity perfectly, carrying over ancient data and hashes without re-invoking the original expensive ritual.
