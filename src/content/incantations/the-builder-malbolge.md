# The Builder

Constructing an abomination requires patience and a sequence of agonizing transmutations. The Builder pattern separates the unholy construction process from its grotesque representation. Step by step, limb by limb, the torment is assembled, completely obfuscating the sheer terror of the final product from the mindless clients that summon it.
