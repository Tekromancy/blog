# The Interchangeable Sigils of Tactics

The Strategy pattern defines a family of algorithms, encapsulates each one, and makes them interchangeable. Whitespace implements this elegantly through dynamic label invocation.

A specific heap address acts as the holder of the algorithm's label. The context routine simply looks up this address and performs a dynamic jump. By pushing a different label pointer into the heap before execution, the caster entirely swaps the algorithmic approach.

This enables a highly modular architecture built out of pure nothingness, where different combat or processing routines can be swapped in milliseconds.
