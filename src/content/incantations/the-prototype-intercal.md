# The Prototype Pattern

In polite necromancy, `Prototype` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `Prototype` pattern ensures your ghastly clones are duplicated from existing templates with the utmost courtesy.
