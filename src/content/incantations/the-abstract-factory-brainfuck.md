The Abstract Factory in Brainfuck is not a struct, but a location on the infinite memory tape. We dictate a region of the abyssal memory as the "Factory". By shifting the pointer, we alter which grotesque entity is summoned. The runes bleed into each other, much like the minds of those who stare too long.
