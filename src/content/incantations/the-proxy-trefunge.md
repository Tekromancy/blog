Before the pointer is permitted to enter the sacred geometries of a highly classified Three-Dimensional Topology Ward, it must bypass the Dimensional Sentinel. This is the Proxy.

Using a probabilistic or conditional gate (`?`), the proxy intercepts the execution thread. Only if the correct dimensional keys are provided does it step into the hyper-layer (`h`) to trigger the real construct. Otherwise, it simply terminates (`@`).
