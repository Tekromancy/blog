# The Iterator

In the darkest kitchens of the cyberpunk underworld, the **Iterator** is not merely coded; it is *cooked*. This profane recipe binds the principles of the Behavioral pattern into a physical manifestation of culinary dread. 

## Arcane Implementation

To navigate the internal structure of a fleshy aggregate (is it a list? a tree? a gelatinous cube?) without risking insanity, one uses an Iterator. The pointing bone acts as the cursor, blindly tapping forward through the grotesque collection of ingredients, extracting them one by one. The chef remains safely ignorant of how the internal organs are arranged.

- **Fleshy Aggregate**: The collection/iterable.
- **Pointing Bone**: The iterator state/cursor.
- **Step Forward**: The `next()` operation.

*Consume at your own risk.*
