The Factory Method acts as a Spatial Spawner within our Three-Dimensional Topology Wards. Rather than hardcoding the ward creation in the primary geometric plane, we use a directional divergence (`?`) to shift the instruction pointer into a specialized layer.

This deferred spatial delegation allows the underlying matrix architecture to remain unchanged while sub-wards handle the specific materialization.
