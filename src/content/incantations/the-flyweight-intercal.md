# The Flyweight Pattern

In polite necromancy, `Flyweight` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `Flyweight` pattern ensures you share intrinsic soul fragments to conserve memory, managing your legions with the utmost courtesy.
