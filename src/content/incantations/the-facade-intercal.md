# The Facade Pattern

In polite necromancy, `Facade` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `Facade` pattern ensures a simple, unified gateway into the convoluted horrors of the crypt, provided with the utmost courtesy.
