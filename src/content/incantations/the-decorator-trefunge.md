When a Three-Dimensional Topology Ward requires rapid reinforcement without altering its base geometry, tekromancers employ the Decorator. This technique involves wrapping the core ward in Layered Warding.

By manipulating the Z-axis, we construct an Aura layer directly above (`h`) the base construct. The instruction pointer pierces through the Aura, absorbing its protective resonance, before executing the base ward below.
