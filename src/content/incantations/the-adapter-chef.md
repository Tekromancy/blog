# The Adapter

In the darkest kitchens of the cyberpunk underworld, the **Adapter** is not merely coded; it is *cooked*. This profane recipe binds the principles of the Structural pattern into a physical manifestation of culinary dread. 

## Arcane Implementation

The Adapter pattern is the macabre art of grafting. When two incompatible interfaces (or biological forms) refuse to communicate, the Adapter steps in as the translator. By forcibly stitching an alien appendage onto the main dish, you allow old clients to interface with new, unspeakable horrors without having to rewrite their core consumption logic.

- **Incompatible Extremity**: The Adaptee class.
- **Surgical Thread**: The wrapping logic.
- **Numbing Poison**: Exception handling for interface mismatches.

*Consume at your own risk.*
