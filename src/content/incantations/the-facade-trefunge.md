The inner workings of Three-Dimensional Topology Wards can drive an unprepared instruction pointer to madness. The Facade pattern creates a plane of Spatial Obfuscation.

It acts as a simplified 2D interface. Only the Facade knows the correct coordinates (`g`) and dimensional shifts (`h`) required to safely navigate the esoteric labyrinth below, shielding the caller from topological horror.
