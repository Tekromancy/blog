When Three-Dimensional Topology Wards become chaotic webs of inter-ward communication, the grid is at risk of spatial collapse. The Mediator acts as the Dimensional Hub.

Rather than wards pinging each other across arbitrary vectors, all signals are sent to the central nexus. This hub uses multi-directional shifts (`^`, `v`, `<`, `>`) and layer transitions (`h`, `l`) to efficiently route arcane traffic, maintaining order in the matrix.
