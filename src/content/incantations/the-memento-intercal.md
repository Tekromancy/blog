# The Memento Pattern

In polite necromancy, `Memento` is essential. When dealing with the dead, you must never forget to say `PLEASE`. If you fail to be sufficiently polite to the compiler, the spell will fail, and your spirits will not awaken. The `Memento` pattern ensures you can preserve the state of an undead servant and rewind its decay with the utmost courtesy.
