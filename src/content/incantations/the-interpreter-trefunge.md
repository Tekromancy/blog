Within the matrix, raw spectral input (`~`) must often be decoded. The Interpreter acts as a Dimensional Lexicon. As characters of the arcane grammar flow into the grid, the Three-Dimensional Topology Ward maps them to specific execution paths.

By chaining conditional evaluations (`= ?`), the geometry itself becomes the syntax tree, dynamically executing complex meta-spells based on the input stream.
