# The Builder: Cybernetic Assembly

Constructing a terminator is not a single incantation—it is a sequence of flesh-grafting and metal-bending. The Builder pattern separates the construction of a complex system from its representation.

In the realm of ArnoldC, where state is tracked with the precision of a hunting machine, we execute subroutines in a specific order to build our digital golem piece by piece. Once the parts are attached, the machine wakes up.
