# The Singleton

In the darkest kitchens of the cyberpunk underworld, the **Singleton** is not merely coded; it is *cooked*. This profane recipe binds the principles of the Creational pattern into a physical manifestation of culinary dread. 

## Arcane Implementation

There can be only one. The Singleton is the ultimate act of culinary hubris—a dish that refuses to be instantiated more than once across the entire runtime of the universe. It is the Lich of design patterns. By sealing its essence into a single phylactery, the Singleton guarantees global access while preventing any other sous-chef from ever whipping up a duplicate. It exists perpetually, consuming memory until the end of days.

- **Phylactery of Boundless Torment**: The static instance variable.
- **Additional Instances**: A null value ensuring uniqueness.
- **Eternal Existence**: The private constructor.

*Consume at your own risk.*
