# The Facade

The Facade is a lie. It provides a simple, unified interface to an incomprehensible, twisted tangle of subsystem logic. Clients gaze at the pleasing surface, entirely unaware of the eldritch machinery churning below, grinding their sanity into dust beneath layers of abstraction.
