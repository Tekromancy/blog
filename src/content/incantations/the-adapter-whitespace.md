# The Adapter of the Silent Interstices

When two incompatible systems of nothingness must interact, the Adapter bridges the gap. By wrapping the expected stack operations and heap reads in a translational layer of Whitespace, we conform the old interface to the new.

The Unseen Sigils serve as the ultimate universal language. Because Whitespace inherently strips away all syntax, the translation process is purely a structural realignment of data flow.

The caster shapes the flow of data, transmuting the layout of the stack so the receiving void-routine comprehends the invocation seamlessly.
