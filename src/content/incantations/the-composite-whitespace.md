# The Fractal Composite of the Void

The Composite pattern treats individual objects and compositions of objects uniformly. In Whitespace, this implies creating a recursive structure on the heap where unseen nodes point to lists of other unseen nodes.

Traversing this silent hierarchy requires a masterful understanding of pointer arithmetic and recursive label invocation. A single call propagates through the fractal network of spaces and tabs, executing operations on every node.

The beauty of the Composite in the realm of the Unseen Sigils lies in its invisible majesty: a massive tree of logic that executes flawlessly while leaving behind a source file that looks entirely blank.
